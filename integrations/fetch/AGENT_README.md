# Fabrary: turn a sentence into a real printed thing

Tell Fabrary what you'd like made and it gets made on a real printer near you.

**What it does**
- Draws three original designs for your request (AI image generation).
- Picks the best free real printer that can make it: a thermal **sticker printer** or an **Instax photo printer**. If one is busy or off, it uses the other and tells you.
- Shows you the exact print file, the printer, the price and the pickup point before anything is made.
- Sends it to the printer when you say yes, and gives you a pickup code.

**Try it**
- "a cute cat sticker"
- "a beautiful colorful dragon print"
- Reply **1**, **2** or **3** to pick a draft, **more** for other options, **yes** to make it, **status** for an update.

Payment is a mock: nothing is charged. Requests are limited per person per day.

Built for Cal Hacks with uAgents and the Agent Chat Protocol; designs by OpenAI image generation, requests understood by Claude.
